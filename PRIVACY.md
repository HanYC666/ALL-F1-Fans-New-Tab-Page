# Privacy Policy

**HYPR//F1 — Formula 1 Command Center & New Tab Page**

Last updated: September 2026

---

## The short version

This extension doesn't collect anything. No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no data sent to me. Everything stays on your own computer.

---

## What data is stored and where

All your stuff — settings, shortcuts, cached F1 session data, and any wallpaper images you upload — is saved locally in your browser using `chrome.storage.local` and `IndexedDB`. It never leaves your device and I never see it.

If you uninstall the extension, Chrome wipes all of that automatically.

---

## External requests the extension makes

**Jolpica F1 API** (`api.jolpi.ca`) — fetches the race schedule and championship standings. This is a public API, no authentication required. Your IP hits their server the same as any normal website visit.

**YouTube search** — when you click the commentary search button, the extension opens a regular YouTube search URL in your browser. That's just a normal YouTube page load. YouTube does whatever YouTube does with it (their own privacy policy applies there, not mine).

That's it. There's no tracking pixel, no telemetry, no error reporting sending data anywhere.

---

## Permissions

The extension only requests the `storage` permission. It cannot read your browsing history, access other tabs, or inject scripts into websites you visit.
